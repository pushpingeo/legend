Earned polish

Review

Approving a layer means you've checked Claude's rules, above all the low-confidence ones, and you're putting your name to them. Approvals stay in this browser.

How the draft style works

  • Symbols stay correct, but faded, in proportion to Claude's confidence, with an orange ring marking them as unreviewed.
  • Lines are dashed and thinned; areas are washed out with an orange dashed edge.
  • A DRAFT stamp stays on the map until every layer in view is approved.
  • The title block says which step a person did and which step AI did, the way drawing offices recorded "drawn by, checked by".
  • Click any feature to see why it got its symbol.
